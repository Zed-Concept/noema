Wording-unchanged proof — normalized word-stream diff

The deliverable was structure only; wording was not to be edited.
normalize.py strips every structural marker that differs between the
two files (heading #, bullets, ordered numbers, ** , backticks, table
pipes and delimiter row, tabs, all whitespace) and emits one word per
line. An empty diff therefore means the prose is identical.

$ python3 normalize.py AGENTS.before.md > before.words   # 713 words
$ python3 normalize.py AGENTS.md        > after.words    # 713 words
$ diff before.words after.words
(no output — streams identical)

VERDICT: PASS — 713 words before, 713 after, zero differences.
Only markdown structure changed.
